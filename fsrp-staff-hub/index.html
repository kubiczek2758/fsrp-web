Staff Conduct

Professional standards expected from every member of the FSRP staff team.

🛡️

Professionalism

  1. Represent FSRP in a professional manner at all times, both in-game and on Discord.
  2. Treat every player, colleague, and visitor with respect, regardless of rank or history.
  3. Never use staff powers for personal gain, revenge, entertainment, or to influence roleplay.
  4. Keep all staff discussions, decisions, logs, and evidence confidential.
  5. Follow the chain of command and escalate issues through the correct channels.
  6. Remain calm and constructive, even when dealing with difficult players or situations.
⚖️

Impartiality & Integrity

  1. Base every decision on facts, server rules, and documented staff policy — not personal feelings.
  2. Do not show favouritism toward friends, departments, or specific community members.
  3. Recuse yourself from any case where you have a conflict of interest.
  4. Document warnings, kicks, bans, and other major decisions so they can be reviewed.
  5. Give players a fair opportunity to explain themselves before issuing punishments.
  6. Accept feedback and correction from higher-ranked staff without argument.

On Duty & Activity Quota

Duty expectations, the 50/50 rule, and the weekly activity requirement.

⏱️

Being On Duty

  1. Be visibly active and approachable whenever you are marked as on duty.
  2. Respond to player reports, pings, and admin calls in a timely manner.
  3. Maintain a professional appearance and attitude while representing the staff team.
  4. Do not remain AFK for extended periods while still marked on duty.
  5. If you need to step away, mark yourself off duty or notify a higher-ranked staff member.
  6. Prioritize moderation, tickets, and player safety over casual roleplay while on duty.
📊

Quota & 50/50 Rule

  1. Every staff member must complete a minimum activity quota of 2 hours per week.
  2. At least 50% of your in-server time should be spent on staff responsibilities.
  3. Casual roleplay is allowed, but staff duties always take priority when calls or issues arise.
  4. During events, peak hours, or high-ticket periods, focus primarily on moderation.
  5. If you cannot meet your quota, inform a leadership member in advance.
  6. Repeated failure to meet activity expectations may result in warnings, suspension, or demotion.

Handling MOS Calls

MOS stands for Member of Staff. These are in-game or Discord calls for staff assistance.

📞

Response Protocol

  1. Acknowledge the call immediately so the reporter knows assistance is on the way.
  2. Gather context from all involved parties before making any decision.
  3. Remain neutral, calm, and professional — do not escalate the situation emotionally.
  4. Remove disruptive players from the active scene when necessary to protect roleplay.
  5. Restore the scene only after the issue has been fully resolved.
  6. If the situation is beyond your authority, escalate it to a higher-ranked staff member.
📝

Documentation & Follow-Up

  1. Log every MOS call in the designated staff channel or ticket system.
  2. Include player names, the rule or behaviour involved, evidence, and the action taken.
  3. Capture screenshots or clips whenever possible to support your decision.
  4. Ask for guidance before issuing severe punishments if you are uncertain.
  5. Follow up on ongoing reports to ensure the issue does not continue.
  6. Keep MOS logs factual and free from personal opinions or bias.

General Staff Guidelines

Daily procedures and reminders that keep the staff team effective and unified.

🎙️

Communication

Use the correct channels for reports, questions, and department coordination. Avoid unnecessary pings and maintain a respectful, professional tone at all times.

🎓

Training & Development

Attend scheduled staff meetings and training sessions. Stay informed about rule updates, new procedures, and changes to server policy.

🤝

Teamwork

Support your fellow staff members, cover for absences when possible, and escalate issues through the proper chain of command.

🔒

Confidentiality

Never share staff-only information, player reports, internal discussions, logs, or evidence outside of authorised staff channels.

Need Backup?

If you are unsure about a call, punishment, or policy, reach out to the leadership team on Discord.
